Privacy Policy.

This policy explains how Mechanicsburg Plumbing Pros ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and protects information when you visit mechanicsburgpaplumbingpros.com or contact us by phone, email, or web form.

When you call, email, or submit information through this site, we may collect your name, phone number, email address, service address, and details about the plumbing issue you are contacting us about. We do not knowingly collect information from children.
