Any suspected gas leak is a leave-the-house-and-call situation, not a wait-and-see one.

Beyond emergency response, gas line work covers new appliance hookups, line extensions for additions like an outdoor kitchen or fire pit, and repair of aging lines in Mechanicsburg's older housing stock. Every gas line job, new or repaired, gets pressure-tested before being put into service, since a line that looks fine visually can still have a slow leak at a fitting.

If You Smell Gas.

What To Do Right Now

Leave The House Immediately.

Do not turn on lights, use a phone inside, or attempt to locate the leak yourself.

Call The Gas Utility From Outside.

Most utilities have a 24-hour emergency line specifically for suspected leaks.

Do Not Return Until Cleared.

Wait for the utility or a technician to confirm it is safe before going back inside.

Call For Repair Once Safe.

Once the immediate hazard is addressed, a technician can locate and repair the actual source.

Common Non-Emergency Work.

Installations And Extensions

New gas range and dryer hookups, water heater conversions from electric to gas, and line extensions to a patio for a grill or fire pit make up most non-emergency gas line calls. Each new connection point requires sizing the line correctly for the appliance's BTU demand. An undersized line starves an appliance of adequate gas pressure even when everything else about the installation is correct.

Appliance DemandHigh-BTU appliances like a whole-home generator need correctly sized supply lines.

A gas water heater installation is often paired with general water heater replacement work, particularly when converting from an electric unit to take advantage of lower gas operating costs. A gas line run for a new tankless unit specifically needs its own sizing calculation, since tankless units draw gas at a higher instantaneous rate than a comparable tank unit.

Older cast-iron gas piping, still found in some pre-war sections of Mechanicsburg, is generally sound but brittle at threaded joints that have not been disturbed in decades. Extending a line from an old iron run sometimes reveals that the existing pipe needs a support bracket or a short transition fitting to modern black steel or CSST tubing. That detail is worth flagging during the initial estimate rather than discovering mid-installation.

Generator hookups deserve a specific mention as a growing category of gas line work in this area, particularly after households in the Cumberland Valley have experienced multi-day power outages during severe storms. A whole-home standby generator has a substantial BTU demand that most existing gas services were never sized to support, which means a generator installation almost always requires a dedicated line sizing calculation, not just a simple tap into the nearest existing run.

A meter upgrade sometimes accompanies a larger gas line project, particularly when adding a generator or a second high-demand appliance to a home whose existing meter and regulator were sized decades ago for a smaller total load. Coordinating that upgrade with the gas utility, rather than discovering the need for it mid-installation, keeps a generator or appliance project on its original schedule instead of stalling on a utility-side approval partway through. A home planning a generator alongside a home business or rental unit on the same property should confirm the meter upgrade covers both uses before either project is finalized.

Common Questions.

Straight Answers

A persistent or strong odor is never normal and warrants immediate action per the safety steps above. A very faint, brief smell during ignition on some older furnace models can be normal, but it is worth having checked rather than assumed safe.

Most township and borough building codes require a permit and inspection for new gas line work, which is typically handled as part of the project rather than left to the homeowner.

A pressure test holds the line at a set pressure for a specified time to confirm no drop occurs, which would indicate a leak at a fitting or joint before the line is ever put into active service.
